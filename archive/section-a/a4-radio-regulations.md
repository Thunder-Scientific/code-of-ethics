# Rule A4 | Radio Regulations

All personnel have at their disposal a handheld radio which can be used to effectively communicate with other personnel across the facility.

Due to these portable radios being very valuable for communication, they have a set of rules when it come to using them to ensure they are used effectively.

When using radios, you are expected to follow all behavioral, in-game, and Roblox ToS rules as with standard communication.

Radios also have their own set of additional rules:

- You may not spam the radios with unnecessary messages
- You may not use the ping feature ( ex. \@‌everyone ) for unnecessary messages. This is reserved for important messages only.
  - How the ping feature works is if you very specifically type out a teams name, it makes your text yellow for them and provides an alert noise. Example: “ \@‌Recontainment Unit There is a breach in S2 “ would be yellow for RECON, allowing them to see it easier.
- You may not provide misinformation or lies on the radio, if you are unsure about something make sure to state so.

There are also certain channels available for certain departments. You may only use said channels for their designed purpose.

Combative channels are to speak about emergencies, callouts, site state, etc… without non-combative personnel seeing the messages. Make sure any important site security operations are done on these channels so that it remains focused.

Staff channels are there to communicate with each other about their duties on site and is typically reserved for coordinating site events.

The Global channel is a channel where you can speak freely about anything.

**IF THE RADIOS ARE BROKEN AND CHANNELS DON’T WORK PROPERLY, YOU MAY IGNORE THE PART ABOVE.**

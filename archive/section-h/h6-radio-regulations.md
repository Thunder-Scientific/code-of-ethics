# Notice H6 | Radio Regulations

All personnel have at their disposal a handheld radio, which can be used to effectively communicate with other personnel at longer ranges and give out callouts for dangerous situations or anything in general.

Due to these portable radios being very valuable for communication, they have set rules when it come to using them, which can be found below.

When using radios, you are expected to follow all TSC and ToS rules, breaking them will warrant an infraction like normal.

Radios also have their own set of rules:

- You may not spam the radios with unnecessary messages
- You may not use the ping feature ( ex. \@‌everyone ) for unnecessary things. This is reserved for important messages only. How the ping feature works is if you very specifically type out a teams name, it makes your text yellow for them. Example: “ \@Recontainment Unit There is a breach in S2 “ would be yellow for RECON, allowing them to see it easier.
- You may not provide misinformation or lies on the radio, if you are unsure about something make sure to state so.

There are also certain channels available for certain departments. You may only use said channels for their designed purpose.

Combative channels are to speak about emergencies, callouts, site state, etc.

Staff channels are there to just communicate with eachother.

Global channel is a channel where you can speak about anything.

**IF THE RADIOS ARE BROKEN AND CHANNELS DON’T WORK PROPERLY, YOU MAY IGNORE THE PART ABOVE.**

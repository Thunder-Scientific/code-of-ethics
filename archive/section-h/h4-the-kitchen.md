# Notice H4 | The Kitchen

As a temporary solution until a dedicated kitchen keycard is created. Test subjects are allowed to freely enter the TSCZ kitchen and cook food as long as they are not hostile to any kitchen staff inside. To ensure this, all test subjects who enter the area are agreeing to be subjected to wanding at any point while inside the kitchen to ensure they have the proper L1 card.

- Any test subject inside with contraband may be terminated.
- Any test subject inside that has no contraband but also no L1 card may be cuffed outside of the kitchen.

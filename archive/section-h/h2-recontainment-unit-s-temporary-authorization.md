# Notice H2 | Recontainment Unit's Temporary Authorization

In the interest of site security, permissions that are granted to Special Operations in Rule B6 are to be temporarily granted to the Recontainment Unit in addition to permissions they already have until the complete restriction on Special Operations is lifted.

The permissions in B6 are as follows:

- SD Special Operations may also enter during an on-going system riot or a raid in addition to breaches and lockdowns. Additionally, if armed inmates are using the ventilation while actively being hostile as a means to attack security, SO may enter to deal with that inmate only.

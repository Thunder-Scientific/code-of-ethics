# Notice H8 | Medical Personnel Exemption

All members of the Medical Department ranked Nurse+ are permitted to enter combat zones at their own risk to assist Security personnel through healing and recovery with the understanding that they should remain behind Security as to not get in the way.

- They may also remain outside of panic shelters at their own risk in order to actively support security personnel on all code levels.
- They may enter areas they normally are not allowed to enter due to their security clearance or non-combatant status as long as they are actively supporting Security personnel with the exception of Sector 4 and Unit 4.
- They may also accompany patrols of Security personnel throughout the facility in order to be readily available to provide aid only when necessary.

Note: This is a temporary exemption as a test.

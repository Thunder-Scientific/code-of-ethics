# Notice H11 | Blended Lethal Drinks Experimental Change

As a minor experimental change to the Code of Ethics the following rule is void for the duration of this executive order:

“For the safety of site personnel, only mixtures that do not cause death may be made with the blender and consumed outside of testing purposes. (Such as Ultimate Coffee, Metal Shrapnel, or Uranium).”

This executive order will stay in affect for 14 days and if no major incident or complaint occur, it will be officially removed from the Code of Ethics. If the rule creates enough contention the existing rule may be only scaled back or left in place.

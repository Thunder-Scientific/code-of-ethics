# Notice H7 | Medical Equipment

As per request of the Medical Department, medical equipment (Splint, Tourniquet, etc), with the exception of the disinfectant, will now count as contraband. The rules are as follow:

- Personnel may not possess medical equipment.
- Personnel are required to dispose of or hand over medical equipment to the nearest medical personnel.
- Personnel may use medical equipment as necessary only when no MD are available nearby, otherwise they must resort to being treated by the medical personnel or the medical staff NPCs.

# Rule C5 | Hostage Regulations

For the safety of non-combatant personnel, during times they are taken hostage the following regulations should be followed to ensure no unnecessary damage is caused to hostages. The following document details these regulations and can be found here:

[https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Iy9Wccg8Pj5t3p5uo5acBRC_Sl1ZlhnqJKshUjipCdY/edit](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Iy9Wccg8Pj5t3p5uo5acBRC_Sl1ZlhnqJKshUjipCdY/edit "smartCard-inline")

Violating any of these regulations can make you liable to receive an infraction.

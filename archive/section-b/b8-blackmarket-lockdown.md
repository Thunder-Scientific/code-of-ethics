# Rule B8 | Blackmarket Lockdown

Following it’s activation the Black Market Lockdown (BM LD) stays on for a maximum of 15 minutes to help hosted events proceed without much interference. After the BM LD has been deactivated, either manually or automatically, a period of 15 minutes must pass before it can be activated again.

The BM LD should only be used sparingly and under specific circumstances to avoid abuse:

- RSD Mass Testing
- RSD U4 Testing
- Other forms of mass-events if TS are being disruptive
  - This includes: Mass checkups, mass tours, mass interviews, rallies, etc.

All activations of the BM LD should be announced via the radio system using an “@combatives ” ping. This is to ensure all security personnel are aware of its activation.

Due to the nature of the events the BM LD should be used for, its use is restricted to L-3+ personnel hosting those events.

- L-4+ personnel may grant permission to lower-ranking personnel within their department to use the BM LD in use-cases outside of mass-events

The BM LD should only be used outside of these circumstances if TS are being so disruptive as to make even normal non-combative events impossible to conduct.

Note: The BM LD should never be used by combative personnel

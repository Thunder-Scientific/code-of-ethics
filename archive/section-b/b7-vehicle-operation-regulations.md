# Rule B7 | Vehicle Operation Regulations

Vehicles may only be used for the transport of all personnel.

Personnel with a clearance level of 2 or higher within the department they are currently deployed as may operate vehicles.

- SD Carts and Security Jeeps may only be driven by combative personnel
- The Buggy, Golf Cart and Civilian Jeep may be driven by both combative and non-combative personnel
- The RECON Cart and BWD cart may only be driven by their respective members
- The Heavy Transport Truck may only be driven by Technical Task Squad operatives
- The Bus and all of its counterparts may only be used for non-combative events or rally events for transport.

Operating any vehicle without the correct clearance or exception to do so will result in punishment.

While operating any vehicle you must not deliberately crash it or recklessly operate it at speeds where it is likely to crash or intentionally run personnel over. You are also required to drive vehicles on roads or dedicated paths for vehicles, and you may not drive them on pedestrian walkways or anywhere where a vehicle does not belong. Small vehicles, like carts and buggies may be driven on walkways and other places as long as the driver of the vehicle is not being disruptive with the vehicle or a risk of harm to others by doing so.

Combatants may bypass vehicle driving regulations at their own discretion. If a combatant is to commit a violation of the vehicle driving regulations they must have their sirens activated. If the combatant must act immediately and cannot activate their sirens they will not be punished as long as there is valid reason for it. Abuse of this will lead to punishment.

To avoid incidents an individual is not to:

- Stand on top of vehicles while they are being operated
- Block the path or lunge in the way of the vehicle while it’s being operated
- Shoot the vehicle unless a hostile has control of it
- Throw anything at the vehicle that make cause damage to it or cause it to crash

The person who possesses the Car Keys of a vehicle is the owner of the vehicle. The owner’s name would be clearly visible on the license plates located on the front and rear of the vehicle.

If someone is found driving a car without permission from the owner, it would be considered a Grand Theft Auto crime unless exigent circumstances exist such as vehicle recovery after being stolen by hostiles or moving it out of the way of the train system or roadway. Combatants may borrow vehicles of others without their permission if it’s needed for emergency purposes only.

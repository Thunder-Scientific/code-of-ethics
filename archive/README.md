# Archive

Retired pages, exported from the Trello board's **archived cards** on 2026-08-31,
at the point the repository took over from the board as the source of truth.

These are kept for reference and provenance only. **They are not part of the
published rulebook** and must be excluded from the site build.

attachments — and so had nothing to migrate:
`Rule F2 | Blackmail & Extortion`, `F5 | Corruption`, `Rule F3 | Corruption`,
`Rule F4 | Obstruction`, `Rule F5| Purposeful Violation`, `Notice H10 | Martial Law`.

(`Ethics Committee Orientation`, `WIP`). Nothing to migrate there either.

## Why these filenames differ from the live pages

Live pages are named by bare rule ID (`section-b/b1.md`). Archived pages keep
descriptive names, because **retired rule IDs get reused**. Six archived cards
carry an ID that a different, current rule now occupies:

| ID | Archived meaning | Current meaning |
|---|---|---|
| D2 | Entering TS CZ | TS Deployment |
| D3 | Entering CIS CZ | Solitary Confinement |
| E3 | Combative Escort Procedures | Misusing Scientific Equipment |
| F2 | Blackmail & Extortion | Solicitation & Conspiracy |
| G1 | Warnings Regulations | Infraction Expungement |
| H10 | Martial Law | State of Siege |

Two archived cards also share the ID `H4` (`The Kitchen`, `Bounty Hunting`).

A citation of "Rule D2" predating this archive therefore means something
different from what `/section-d/d2/` serves today. Worth knowing when reading
old mod logs or appeals.

# Rule E3 | Combative Escort Procedures

Combatives should only ever decline escorting a test if they have a valid reason. Valid reasons for declining a test escort are as follows:

- The RsD requesting the test is an Assistant Researcher or if the MD requesting a checkup is below the rank of Nursing Student without a supervisor
- The TSCZ/CISCZ would not be properly secured without the combative in question (At least 2 security must remain inside TSCZ to guard)
- The only available combatives do not have the necessary tools to secure the test
- There is currently an identified CIS or breached specimen in the TSCZ or around it
- The situation in TSCZ is nearing a riot OR there are multiple problematic TS causing problems within the TSCZ
- There is currently an event in which SD personnel are partaking UNLESS said event involves testing
- There is an identified breached and confirmed hostile specimen somewhere in the CTZ
- There is an ongoing sitewide emergency i.e.: Breach, Raid, Riot, CTZ Lockdown, etc...

If a combative is seen denying test escorts for minuscule reasons, such as a personal grudge against the RsD in question, they will get infracted by EC. However, said combative may be given more leniency on that, provided they have a good reason which is not specified on this list. If the reason provided by the combative is seen as not fit, the combative will be infracted.

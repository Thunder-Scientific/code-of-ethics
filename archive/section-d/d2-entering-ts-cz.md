# Rule D2 | Entering TS CZ

The TSCZ is a place where you will find most of the usually volatile Test Subjects, and because of their nature regulations below have been put in place to prevent some casualties.

The TSCZ starts from the break room exit to the inner TSCZ, and goes all through up until the entrance to the pool locker room which is located past the bathrooms. The TSCZ also extends to the gate to the Banquet Hall and the doorway to the kitchen. These locations have additional requirements for test subjects to enter which will be listed later in this section.

Personnel should not enter the TSCZ unless they match at least one of the following:

- A combatant.
- A member of EC, ISB, or ER.
- Personnel of security clearance of 3 or higher.
- Personnel of security clearance of 2 may only enter for up to 10 minutes in order to do their departmental duties or perform simple tasks such as asking questions or letting others know of something. This 10 minute timer may be enforced by security personnel guarding TSCZ, but is recommended to be left to EC to handle it. Loitering with no business being inside is not permitted.

In the interest of safety and minimal distractions for security personnel, non-combatants should stay behind the red combative line unless there is a reasonable excuse to pass. Personnel who decide to cross it are to do so at their own risk and are recommended to have a security escort to ensure their safety.

Security personnel withhold the right to request non-combatants to leave the inner TSCZ, or TSCZ altogether if they are causing a disruption or are being a hazard to others.

- Exceptions include EC, ISB, ER, AD, and mod personnel if necessary to perform their duties.

CIP should not pass the red line during an active breach due to a high chance of being misidentified or injured from anti-biohazard tools. An immune security escort is recommended to ensure their safety or clean up a potential biohazard.

- Special note: Under no circumstances can a CIP pass the red line during an active breach of Specimen-239.

---

Test subjects may freely enter the Banquet hall. The Medbay located next to the banquet hall may only accessed by subjects who are undergoing a valid medical examination.

The TSCZ Kitchen may be freely accessed by test subjects who carry a kitchen-clearance card. Any subject who does not have a kitchen-clearance card is prohibited from entering the kitchen. Any personnel authorized to freely enter the TSCZ may freely access the kitchen, but is recommended to have a security escort due to the ease of access by test subjects.

The pool locker room and poolroom are able to be entered freely by test subjects but it is recommended that combatants actively protect it when there are a large group of subjects using the pool. Any of the hallways past the poolroom are restricted to test subjects.

The medical station inside the TSCZ Commons is restricted from subject access at all times and medical personnel should have at least 1 combatant present to ensure their safety inside the station.

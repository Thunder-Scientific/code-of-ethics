# Rule D3 | Entering CIS CZ

The Contained Infected Subject Containment Zone (CISCZ) houses mostly docile specimen that are selected to be further studied by the corporation. Their docile nature does not eliminate their biohazard status and must still be handled with extra caution. The following rules apply to the CISCZ and by extension the Unit 3 area when CIS are present:

- Only personnel with a level 3 clearance or higher may enter the CISCZ.
- Non-combatants are permitted to remain inside the CISCZ on the top floor only when there’s at least one combatant present in the CISCZ. If the combatant leaves then the non-combatants must also leave until there is another present within the CISCZ. Personnel may enter to determine if there is a combatant present, but must leave once they find that there is none inside. This rule extends to Unit 3 only when CIS are present outside the CISCZ in Unit 3. Non-combatants are then to stay behind the yellow line when entering U3.
- Combatants with partial or full infection immunity are allowed to enter the bottom floor of the CISCZ for any reason.
- Non-combatants may only enter the bottom floor of the CISCZ with permission of the present combatants and an escort of at least 2 partially or fully immune to infection combatants.
- CIP (Contained Infected Personnel) and immune non-combatants may enter the bottom floor of CISCZ as they please as long as there is at least 1 combatant present within the CISCZ to watch over them.
- Non-immune combatants are not allowed on the bottom floor of the CISCZ unless they are given explicit permission by present Recontainment Unit personnel for a valid reason, or other immune combatants if RECON is absent. If no Immune combatants are present to grant permission then the bottom floor may be entered if it’s required to fulfill their duties with good reason. Duties are enumerated within the combatant's departmental handbook or GoI Contract and should not deviate from them unless there is an immediate exigent circumstance to do so. They may roam around the entirety of Unit 3 freely even with CIS presence.
- Combatants reserve the right to tell non-combative personnel to leave the CISCZ or Unit 3 if they are acting in a disruptive manner or are a hazard to themselves, the CIS, or others. If their safety is threatened by imminent or ongoing inmate aggression then they may also by told to leave.
- RECON has the highest authority within the CISCZ, and Unit 3 when CIS are present, during code green and blue. For all other codes or reasons RECON members are to refer to the Security Department Chain of Authority. Additionally, RECON are permitted to order any non-infection immune combatants out during aforementioned cases with good reason.
- RSD, U&M, EC, and ER may bypass the clearance level restriction to enter CISCZ if it's required to fulfill their duties.

The CISCZ officially begins at its door entrance within the Unit 3 Containment zone area. The top floor of the CISCZ begins from the door entrance and ends at the balcony overlooking the bottom floor, CISCZ Solitary, and the Observation room. Anything beyond that is classed as the “bottom floor”.

Unit 3 officially begins at the roadway gate and 2 side doors and ends at the CISCZ entrance and the Unit 4 checkpoint.

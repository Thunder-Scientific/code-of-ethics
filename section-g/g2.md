# G2 | Baseline Penalty

#### **Infractions**

- First Infraction: First verbal warning.
- Second Infraction: First suspension which is 2 days.
- Third Infraction: Second suspension which is 4 days.
- Fourth Infraction: Third suspension which is 1 week.
- Fifth Infraction: Fourth suspension which is 2 weeks.
- Sixth Infraction: Exiled from department(s), may not rejoin until a week has passed.
  - Following the week, the user may **not** be reinstated into their department/rank. They are to re-do intake and rank up as if they are starting fresh.
- Seventh Infraction: Appealable blacklist after 6 weeks.
- Eighth Infraction: Unappealable blacklist.

Everything above is global and applies to all departments the violator is in.

The EC member who wrote your infraction MUST inform you of the infraction and what you have been infracted for. Ethics personnel must of had valid evidence at the time of posting for your punishment or you may not be punished.

Infractions that were valid at the time of posting are still considered valid even if the evidence expires or is no longer accessible.

Note: A2 infractions carry an additional minimum 1-week suspension in addition to the punishment for the infraction number given, it can range to an exile depending on severity (Example: Heavy sexual references, engagement in the aforementioned…).

---

#### **Warnings**

- Warnings are a reduced punishment if an infraction is deemed too severe for the offense.
- Warnings never expire and stay on record forever. EC members who issued your warning must inform you of it and what you're being warned for.
- Warnings cannot be issued for violations of the following sections:  **A2**, **F1**, **F2, and C3** (Only applies to the “Leaked Assets” section.) Violation of those sections is considered a serious offense.
- If you're a repeated offender (6+ warnings and/or 3+ _active_ infractions on record), the Ethics Committee reserves the right to issue an infraction, instead of a warning, even for minor violations.

---

### Appeals

You have the right to appeal your infraction if you think the verdict you have been given is wrong. If you wish to submit an appeal, you must open an infraction expungement ticket.

- You may appeal infractions at any time.
- You must provide all details regarding your infraction if possible.
- You must provide reasons as to why you believe your infraction should be appealed.

---

### Unappealable Infractions

An infraction will become entirely impossible to appeal if:

- It is an A2 infraction
- It is a severe enough F2 infraction. (ex: going rogue)
- If you have gained 4 infractions in two weeks or less, then all of those are considered unappealable.
- You have 8 or more active infractions in total.
- If you have violated your voluntary suspension terms without prior notice of withdrawing, then the infraction you were attempting to expunge will become unappealable for 30 days and will grant a G2 infraction as per the Suspension rules.
- You constantly appeal infractions and then continue stacking them up, or you have gained 4 infractions in four weeks or less. An Ethics Committee vote may be launched against you to deem your infractions unappealable.

---

### Suspensions

##### **Mandatory Suspensions**

These suspensions are given according to how many infractions one has. (This is outlines at the top of this section) While on this suspension, there are rules to deployment and are heavy punishments if it is violated;

- If you’re found to have violated a _mandatory_ suspension, your punishment will immediately rise to an exile from all departments and an **appealable global blacklist**.
- While on mandatory suspension, you **may NOT** have supervised deployment on departmental/GoI teams.
- While on mandatory suspension, you **may NOT** deploy on any teams (including Test Subject, Civilian, and CIS) EXCEPT for Facility personnel and Administrative Department.

##### **Voluntary Suspension**

These suspensions are given when one is attempting to expunge their infractions. However there are still rules for deploying even when the suspension is voluntary;

- While on voluntary suspension, you **may have** supervised deployment on departmental/GoI teams.
- While on voluntary suspension, you **may NOT** deploy on any teams (including Test Subject, Civilian, and CIS) EXCEPT for Facility personnel and Administrative Department UNLESS you have properly requested and are receiving proper supervision. (More information about this in G1)
- If you are found to be violating a _voluntary_ suspension it will make your infraction (that you were attempting to expunge) un-expungable for another 30 days and will also grant a G2 infraction.

---

#### **Zero Tolerance Policy**

A zero tolerance policy (ZTP) is a period of intensive scrutiny by the ethics committee where receiving an infraction or significant departmental punishment will result in you receiving a global blacklist. They can span from 1 week to 3 months long. They may be implemented for the following reasons:

- When appealing a blacklist.
- An Ethics Committee vote launched against you to place you on a ZTP.

---

#### **Global Blacklist**

A global blacklist (GBL) is a form of punishment that exiles you from all TSC departments, preventing you from rejoining any of them until it is appealed. You may find yourself on a global blacklist for the following reasons:

- You have 7 infractions (appealable), or 8+ infractions (unappealable).
- You have gone rogue (e.g.: shooting multiple staff members, running from EC/ISB constantly, and so on). This applies to all corporation teams.
- Found to be exploiting or otherwise violating serious ROBLOX ToS rules.
- Violating a mandatory suspension.

While globally blacklisted, there are certain rules that apply. This includes;

- You may not join the game on any department/division or personnel teams that have access to personnel equipment such as radios, wands, and anything not normally accessible by TS.
  - CIS is considered departmental, therefore globally blacklisted users may not join it.
  - Development teams follow their internal guidelines.
  - TSE and Maxiburger have the exception where their HiCOMM are allowed to decide whether or not a globally blacklisted user may join.
- Users who are a part of an External GoI and are Globally blacklisted, may not deploy at all.

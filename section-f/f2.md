# Rule F2 | Solicitation & Conspiracy

The attempt to or having another individual plan to perform a harmful action against other people (test subject or personnel) with malicious intent through means of:

- Hiring hitmen or setting bounties that grant rewards of any type.
  - An exemption is made for the SD Assignments board as well as the KOS orders from both SD and Maxiburger.
  - Maxiburger KOS orders can only be fulfilled within the Maxiburger establishment.
- Personnel planning to intentionally violate the CoE including when teamed on TS without authorization.
- Personnel planning on purposefully disrupting site events including when teamed on TS without authorization.
- Making serious threats against other personnel (i.e. threatening to terminate personnel in order to disrupt their duties).
- Using or publishing information from personnel only communication channels with the intent to aid hostile forces (i.e. streaming staff communications to users on Test Subject or otherwise hostile teams).

---

Personnel who are found performing any of the actions below are to be deemed as “going rogue” (referred to as a rogue). Suspected rogues may be apprehended by any personnel that can safely capture them and be reported to an Ethics Committee Agent as soon as possible:

- Personnel dropping or willfully giving access to equipment or tools to TS/CIS/CIV or otherwise hostile forces (this includes Maxiburger staff, despite them being a neutral force).
  - Putting departmentally issued tools/weapons, including decoy rifles, into green bins falls under this rule as a violation, they are to be placed in red bins.
- Personnel aiding or abetting TS/CIS/CIV, or otherwise hostile forces, with escaping or accessing unauthorized locations. This includes leaking site operations to hostile forces.
- Personnel breaking multiple Code of Ethics rules and/or breaking multiple sections of the same rule.
- Unauthorized personnel in possession of the Nuclear Authentication Disk (NAD) or Nuclear Access Key without any valid reason, or refusing to report discovery of/give it to L5+ Security Personnel.
- Personnel attempting to terminate multiple other personnel without proper justification.
- Personnel wearing disguises to break CoE and act as though they were a disguised Test Subject.
- Personnel causing mass site distress through means of the control room.
- Personnel intentionally infecting themselves while deployed on a departmental team through a puddle, a specimen chamber or by any other means. (The only exception to this is RAH hybridizing themselves, following their internal regulations)
- Personnel causing/planning an event to go wrong to instill chaos. (example: deliberate disregard for precaution letting armed, infected, or irradiated subjects run loose.)
- Any other action the Ethics Committee determines to be of significant damage to the corporation.
- Violating a **mandatory** suspension. See G2 for more information on mandatory suspensions.

Personnel determined to have gone rogue are to be punished with a global blacklist upon infraction and are to be logged in the Ethics Committee Roster:

[https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1p95f9DuNZg3LTULuYZ8AasUpdyh1Cc8Cg0IrdTemML4/edit?gid=1089969201#gid=1089969201](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1p95f9DuNZg3LTULuYZ8AasUpdyh1Cc8Cg0IrdTemML4/edit?gid=1089969201#gid=1089969201 "smartCard-inline")

People who have been found to go rogue on drafted personnel teams are only liable to being globally blacklisted if they otherwise hold a clearance. People who go rogue on drafted teams who do **not** hold clearance will be blacklisted from future draft events. (Also applies to standard infractions.)

(Note: In specific instances punishment may vary based on the consensus of EC)

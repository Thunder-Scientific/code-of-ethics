# Rule F1 | Loopholes & Exploitations

F1 infractions are almost if not always **added onto** original punishments as another infraction depending on the situation.
Exploiting loopholes within any rules will result in harsher punishment(s) depending on the rule broken.
Lying to intelligence personnel will make you liable for an F1 infraction;

- Lying in an attempt to incriminate an innocent person or to try and get out of your original punishment will result in a harsher verdict.
- Lying to intelligence to intervene with their duties will also make you liable for an F1 infraction.

Violators intentionally evading intelligence personnel while they are engaged in their official duties will be met with a F1 infraction on top of their original punishment. (Including but not limited to; leaving while being questioned, running from intel personnel, etc.)

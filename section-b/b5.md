# Rule B5 | PA, Vent & Contingency System Guidelines

#### PA System {#pa-system}

The global PA system is only to be used to send server-wide announcements by L4+ personnel, GoI Command members or by Special Operations MRs regarding any lock down procedure.
Additionally, whilst using the PA you are required to speak in a professional and appropriate manner.
You may only use the PA system to announce the following:

- The start of a raid, breach, riot and/or lock down.
- Ordering non combative personnel to a designated safe area when the proper codes are applied
- Declaring a site evacuation during a Code Delta or Code Amber
- High ranking combative personnel ordering their subordinates
- MD High ranks announcing the start of a Scenario Event
- JU members announcing a JU designation
- Combatives may announce when the PA gets retaken from a hostile force, professionalism rules must still be followed.
- Psychology Branch may use the PA to announce hosted events such as group therapy.
- Research and Studies personnel may use the PA to request for assisting users (other RSD, combatives, etc.) for testing purposes.
  - People who utilize the PA for this reason must be sure to send a clear and to the point message.

‌

Combatives, regardless of rank, must use the PA to announce the start and end of a sectoral lockdown, including CTZ lockdowns.

#### TSCZ local PA {#tscz-local-pa}

The local PA in the TSCZ may not be used disruptively and is to be used for announcing non-combative events.
It may also be used to announce TSCZ lockdowns instead of the global PA.

#### TSCZ Lockdown {#tscz-lockdown}

In the event of a threat/incident affecting the Test Subject Containment Zone, the lock down of the TSCZ may be used in various ways and may only be used for the following reasons:

- A breached specimen is inside the TSCZ or ventilation systems connected to it, and it has not been contained for 20 minutes. If the specimen is actively causing a mass infection in the TSCZ it may be turned on.
- Any amount of radiation has breached containment and is creating a risk of harming Test Subjects or Personnel.
- A large amount of inmates have been constantly rioting for 5+ during Code Gamma+, which cannot be handled by normal security forces.
- TSCZ has been overrun by Hostile GOI's for 5 minutes.
- A large number of hostiles are causing a disruption within the ventilation system, and a Security Force (full combative) HR+, GoI Command Member, or Special Operations MR authorizes a ventilation sweep. Before initiating a lock down, you must warn inmates via the PA system prior to activating it.
- It has been Code Red for 45+ minutes, or Code Gamma for 5+ minutes

The TSCZ Riot Gate, can be engaged by anyone under these circumstances:

- TSCZ is unguarded or taken over.
  - Can also be used if understaffed (less then 2 combatives)
- Code Red+.
- Mass infection is happening in TSCZ during a breach.
- A system riot is occurring.
- RSD is starting any kind of test
  - The Riot Gate must be opened after the briefing room is cleared

#### Ventilation {#ventilation}

A set of universal rules for who can enter into any ventilation;

- Juggernaut Units whenever they deem necessary.
- Special Operations during on-going system riots, raids as well as breaches and lock downs, or when armed inmates are using the ventilation shafts as cover.
  - “Using as cover” would be if an inmate is actively showing hostility and running to the vents for safety.
- Combatant units entering the ventilation shaft to finish off a downed hostile that they were previously chasing. You must immediately leave after doing so.

##### **TSCZ Black market/vents** {#tscz-black-market-vents}

Vents that are connected to the TSCZ black market may be accessed during these conditions along with previously stated;

- Fumigators or Combatives with partial/full immunity during a confirmed breach within the area, to sweep for any potential or confirmed biohazards.
  - Immunes that enter the Black market without an authorized vent sweep, during a breach under this rule, may ONLY engage in combat with _infected subjects_ and not anyone else outside of the Self Defense clause.
- Combative units during TSCZ lockdowns that are **not** caused by a breach.
- TSC based Security force (Security Department, RECON, SET, etc.) in groups of 2, when armed inmates are using the ventilation shafts as cover when there are no Special Operations present.

Exhaust vent below the TSCZ Black-market is considered NOT connected to the Black-market, when the BM LD is off.
When the BM LD is on, exhaust is then considered “connected to BM”.
Stacking to get into the BM, or to get a shot into the BM vent, is not allowed. (Unless one of the requirements is met for you to be allowed within the BM ventilation.)

##### **Additional Black Market Vents** {#additional-black-market-vents}

Other black market vents such as "Liams" (Sector 2 black market above Sector 2 commons), the vending machine market, and surface black market, all follow the non-TSCZ specific rules in the points above. However, additional conditions apply for _**these areas specifically**_ such as;

- Special operations may enter vents connected to black markets alone
- Immunes may freely enter CTZ Vending Machine market.
- Combative units in groups of 2 may enter the shaft to deal with any confirmed threat within it, loitering inside is prohibited.

The "tram blackmarket" is not considered "blackmarket ventilation". If there are confirmed hostiles within it, combative units may clear it out but must exit immediately after.

##### **Ventilation not connected to Black markets** {#ventilation-not-connected-to-black-markets}

Many ventilation systems found around the site are not connected to any high-traffic areas such as black markets however they do still have their own set of rules.

- These systems may be accessed by combative units freely.
- Non-combative personnel for the use of shorter routes/travel between locked down areas.

##### **CISCZ Black market vents** {#ciscz-black-market-vents}

The ventilations system within CISCZ is entirely connected to the black-market since there are 3 locations the black-market rotates through. All vents in and surrounding the CISCZ are to be considered “connected to a black-market” as there is no way to pre-determine where the black-market spawns.

#### TSCZ Black-market Lockdown {#tscz-black-market-lockdown}

In some special circumstances, the black market lock down for the TSCZ may be enabled by non-combative personnel. This LD may stay on for a maximum of 15 minutes, and has a cool down of 15 minutes to be turned on again.
Classrooms (Seminar wing) lockdown has the same restrictions as the BM Lockdown and can be activated as well if conditions below are met. (It may still be closed when S2 Partial is engaged.)

The LD may only be enabled if the following conditions apply:

- Non-combative personnel (L3+) hosting a mass event (Mass interview, mass checkup, etc.)
- RsD Organizing/Starting any type of test

Due to the nature of the events the BM LD should be used for, its use is restricted to L-3+ personnel hosting those events.

- L-4+ personnel may grant permission to lower-ranking personnel within their department to use the BM LD in use-cases outside of mass-events

All activations of the BM LD should be announced via the radio system using an “@combatives ” ping. This is to ensure all security personnel are aware of its activation.

Note: The BM LD should never be used by combative personnel. (Except during raids)

#### Sector 1 Med-bay Lockdown {#sector-1-med-bay-lockdown}

In the event of an ongoing Site Emergency, Medical personnel may lockdown the Sector 1 Med-bay for the purpose of keeping all occupants inside safe. During this time it may act as a safe area just as a panic shelter would.

The following criteria for a lockdown are as listed:

- There is a confirmed threat inside of Sector 1.
- The current site code is set to GAMMA or above.

When medical personnel are to lockdown the Med-bay, it needs to be announced over Radio for other staff members or Security personnel.

The Sector 1 Med-bay may also be locked down by the order of MD HR+ without needing any of the 2 previous criteria's being met.

#### Sector Lockdown {#sector-lockdown}

In the event of a threat/incident affecting S1/S2, a lock down of the area may be used in various ways and may only be used for the following reasons:

- A breached specimen has been confirmed to be located within the sector/is confirmed to be heading to the sector.
- A large amount of inmates have breached the sector, and combatants have lost control of it for over 10 minutes.
  A full lock down of the sector in the event of a mass breach or a Code Gamma+ Test Subject riot, and if there is an L5+, GoI SHR+, or Combative/combative divisional HR+ to monitor/control the lock down usage.
  - The definition of a full sector lock down is closing all of the window grates and all blast doors within S2. In S1, a full sector LD would be closing all blast doors within Sector 1.

Standalone usage of blast doors or window graters are not allowed unless there is a **Code Red+** active. Combatants may modify the lock downs of the sectors if they deem it necessary.

After the following threats have been dealt with, or procedures are done, the lock down must be turned off immediately/procedure must be concluded. **The TSCZ lock down may not be enabled during system riots as it has been ordered by the Site Administrator.**

### CTZ lockdown {#ctz-lockdown}

In the event of a CTZ containment breach or other hostiles within the sector, the CTZ lockdown may be enabled, locking the 2 main entrances, either trapping hostiles inside or keeping them out. The CTZ LD may only be used for the following reasons:

- 3+ hostiles enroute to the sector (tram BM onwards)
- 3+ hostiles are inside of the sector
- Any amount of hostiles are enroute using vehicles
- Any amount of hostiles are inside of the sector with a vehicle
- A breach is taking place in the sector
- May also be turned on if requested by an RSD due to either riskiness of a test, or to keep a large scale test in CTZ safe
  - a "risky" test would be defined as a test that may require large amounts (3-5) of infected to be let loose in CTZ for the test. Or if U4 specimens are being used in CTZ (outside of U4)
  - Large scale tests would be a mass test using any large area of CTZ as their "test chamber"
- JU operatives whenever they deem necessary, given they can provide valid reasoning

After the incident that caused the lockdown is resolved, the lockdown may be left on for an additional 5 minutes to ensure no more problems arise, after which it is to be turned off.

### CTZ contingency procedures {#ctz-contingency-procedures}

#### Bridge operation {#bridge-operation}

During any and all codes, the CTZ Bridge must remain up, regardless of code. Any and all personnel should announce when they're using the CTZ Bridge, to avoid confusion if a hostile threat is using the bridge.
If a security checkpoint is being run by the bridge (Inner CTZ, main bridge, CTZ entrance road) it may remain down upon request of the unit guarding the closest checkpoint to it.

#### Chamber Lockdowns {#chamber-lockdowns}

Individual chambers may only be locked down in these instances:

- If an active breach had been just cleared of that specific specimen.
  - Example: There was a 990 breach that was just cleared, the 990 chamber can be put in lockdown.
- A large group (4-6) of TS have been spotted heading to CTZ in attempt to breach.

May stay on until the immune combatives have deemed it safe for it to be unlocked down.

---

### Blackout {#blackout}

Deactivation of the site’s lighting may be utilized as a last resort effort from site security to regain stability of the site. The authority to enable the blackout protocol is only given to high ranking security force members (L4+ or GoI officer rank equivalent), specialized unit MR+ (Special Operations and the Recontainment Unit) and the Juggernaut Unit.

Before the blackout protocol is enabled, it must be announced on the PA system, stating who authorized it. The protocol may be announced in the name of the authorizing officer but the said officer must be on-duty (in-game.) The following are valid reasons for the blackout protocol to be enabled:

- An armed riot has spread outside of the TSCZ, or a breach has made it out of the CTZ which has caused the loss of a sector and has not been contained within 25 minutes.

The lights may only be turned off for up to 15 minutes before they must be turned back on again. If the situation which has caused the blackout has not yet been handled, the time is extended until the threat is eliminated. **Blackouts may not be used in System Riots.**

**Note:** The lights should never be turned off during raids due to the vast majority of hostiles being equipped with night-vision which would heavily reduce the effectiveness of allied security forces.

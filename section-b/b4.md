# Rule B4 | Tool Usage

Tools ranging from wands, cuffs, etc. are all part of loadouts for certain personnel. To ensure these tools are used appropriately, certain restrictions apply.

### Cuffs {#cuffs}

Personnel may use cuffs for certain reasons, whether it be on inmates or other personnel. When they do use them, the following restrictions apply.

Personnel may not do the following actions on cuffed individuals:

- Threaten to drop the individual over harmful or deadly ledges
- Infect/ Have others infect the cuffed/carried individual unless in a test
- Give contraband to the individual
- Kill or intentionally harm the cuffed/carried individual, unless:
  - The cuffed/carried individual is an inmate that is being brought to solitary confinement or a test/event and they must be stunned to avoid risks of escape before putting them in Solitary Confinement, or a large weapon is found on them and they must be terminated.
  - The cuffed/carried individual is an inmate undergoing a test and the test requires it to minimize collateral damage while disposing of the Test Subject. This also applies to any inmates who misbehave during a test and need to be terminated.
  - The cuffed individual is an inmate who is actively using other people as cover after attacking personnel.
  - The cuffed individual is wearing any disguise regardless if no contraband is possessed.
  - The carried individual is an armed inmate who has been downed in the TSCZ and was taken to a safer location to finish them off to ensure minimal collateral damage and the securing of the subject's weapons.

Inmates who were found glitching may be cuffed, but it is recommended you report these actions to the in-game moderation team and leave it to them. Additionally, you’re permitted to cuff exploiters as long as you have evidence of them exploiting, but you must be careful not to restrain moderators under this clause.

Infected specimens may not be cuffed unless the said infected is one of the following:

- An unarmed CIS.
- An infected within the CIS-CZ.
- Being brought back from/during an event with the host's permission. Detainment rules from the section above apply whilst they are cuffed.
- An Armed CIS may be cuffed if:
  - The CIS is in or walking away from a restricted area.
  - The CIS is walking away from an open BM vent.
  - The CIS was seen out of containment but has returned to containment.
  - The CIS is seen binning, wielding, dropping, or has contraband visibly.
  - The CIS is near another CIS who has dropped contraband.
  - Additionally, combatives must attempt to allow them to willingly drop their weapons before apprehending them.
  - Combatives may cuff and wand CIS if they have reasonable suspicion of them carrying any concealed weaponry.
- CIS that use the '/e surrender' emote upon being confronted may be cuffed regardless of weapons or contraband.

Personnel may only be cuffed/carried when:

- They have been downed and must be relocated to a safe area to revive them.
- They have gone rogue.
- You are a part of EC or ISB.
- Said personnel are your subordinates in your current department.
- You have received explicit permission from a DI+ of the department member you’re cuffing.
- You have received permission from the person you're cuffing.

You may not use your cuffs pointlessly or disruptively whether it’s an inmate or personnel that you’re restraining.

### Wand {#wand}

The wand is a tool that is typically part of the arsenal of combatant personnel or intelligence personnel to check if inmates and/or personnel are in any possession of contraband. You must not use the wand pointlessly or disruptively and follow the listed regulations while using it:

- Checking inmates for contraband if they are acting suspiciously. (Covering up their back constantly, having been near a TS who had contraband, avoiding security presence, etc…)
- Wanding other personnel for testing purposes.
- Checking inmates for contraband freely if the respective protocol is active in your department. (Including SD contraband sweep drill.)
- Confiscating any type of contraband including lethal drinks when they are being brought to solitary confinement.
- Checking personnel for unauthorized firearms or to verify their identity.

Do not use the wand to obtain undroppable items (such as special keycards, tools, weapons, etc.), or to deliberately drop every single item from one's inventory.

### Grenades {#grenades}

Throwables can be vital for support in active combat. To ensure they do not get misused and for the safety of other personnel, the following regulations should be followed when using throwables:

- Utility Grenades (Flashbangs, Smoke Grenades) should not be randomly or disruptively used.
- Harmful throwables (e.g. grenades, explosives) should not be randomly deployed intentionally.
- Frag Grenades should only be used if the EOP is Code Red+ or higher.
- Sterilizers should not be randomly or disruptively used and also should not be used in combination with any form of fire unless authorized or there is no clear and present danger to surrounding personnel.

### Radio {#radio}

The radio is a vital communication tool available for all personnel to freely use. When using a radio, the user is expected to follow all behavioral, in-game and Roblox ToS rules as with standard communication.

Radios also possess their own set of additional rules:

- Radios may not be used for irrelevant communication during emergencies or during exchange of important information.
- You may not provide misinformation or lies on the radio, if you are unsure about something make sure to state so.
- You may not use the ping feature ( ex. \@‌everyone ) for unnecessary messages. This is reserved for important messages only.

The ping feature of the radio is similar in nature to the one on Discord, simply type in a users or teams name after a “ \@ “ and the user/team will be pinged alongside your message. You may also use \@‌Combatives to ping all combatants and \@‌everyone to ping everyone which possesses a radio.

#### Channels {#channels}

The radio has a few channels in its disposition, each channel having a different purpose.

- Global channels may be used freely by anyone for any reason.
- Staff channels are reserved for staff/event coordination.
- Combatant channels are reserved for callouts, emergencies and other site security topics (like SC strikes)
- _The existence of any other channel is a myth._

---

### Pagers & CSM Terminals {#pagers-and-csm-terminals}

Communication & Shift Manager terminals are another way to establish communication between personnel alongside radios, they also come with a pager. Any message transmitted from the CSM terminal is received by personnel's pager within a 200 meters radius of said terminal.

Pagers and CSM terminals follow a set of rules that is nearly identical to the rules of radio usage. You must follow behavioral rules and don't be disruptive.

---

### Medical equipment {#medical-equipment}

Medical equipment (Splint, Tourniquets, etc) is found in various locations where medical personnel work or are commonly found. The equipment is generally restricted for use by personnel who are not designated medical staff. The rules are as follows:

- Personnel may not possess medical equipment.
- Personnel are required to dispose of or hand over medical equipment to the nearest medical personnel.
- Personnel may use medical equipment as necessary only when no MDs are available nearby, otherwise, they must resort to being treated by the medical personnel or the medical staff NPCs.
- Disinfectants may be carried and used in the case of an emergency by personnel at risk of infection.

---

### Miscellaneous {#miscellaneous}

Personnel are prohibited from intentionally destroying their departmental or GOI-issued non-weapon equipment in any manner. (examples: ID papers, Cuffs, Keycards, Radio, etc.)

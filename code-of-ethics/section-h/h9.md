# Notice H9 | 2nd Amendment Rights

All non-combatants (except for RAH unpaired assets) are allowed to carry the AR15/M16 and SPAS-12, acquired from either the SD armory or Armed & Dangerous. Non-combatants do not require a permit for these weapons, but still may not carry other weaponry without a permit.

- Contained Infected Personnel (CIP) with over 200 health are allowed to carry Pistols corresponding to your Firearm Permit rank and you are NOT allowed to grab the AR15/M16 or SPAS-12.

Non-combatants may fire at any hostiles shooting at any other personnel, including combatants.
Non-combatants are still NOT allowed to engage in active combat or chase after hostiles. Self defense regulations still apply.

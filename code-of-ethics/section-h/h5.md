# Notice H5 | Train Operation

Personnel with a clearance level of 3 or higher within the department they are currently deployed as may operate the train system freely. The exception to this is U&M Engineers, Recon Operatives, and EC Junior Board Agents being able to operate the train system freely as well.

Operating the train without the correct clearance or exception to do so would be in violation of Rule B1.

While operating the train system you must not deliberately crash the train or recklessly operate it at speeds where it is likely to crash, or intentionally run personnel over with the train. Doing so will be in violation of Rule B3 or C1 respectively.

# Notice H10 | State of Siege

On Blue+, Any Semi and Full combative team may grab any SD weapon. Except for the M4 and grenades. (ISB, IOS, TTS, BP). Semi combatives are also considered full combatives during this time.

- Internal regulations/CoE are to be followed still with other throwables. (Flashbangs, molotovs, etc.)
- SD are to still follow rules regarding M4 and grenade usage.
  Any Semi and Full combative team are immediately dispatched to help the site regain stability, voiding their additional duties. (CM, TTS, ISB, BP, IOS, RAH paired or unpaired). JU and RAH are only to be deployed during Red+ and they must undeploy immediately upon the EoP being lowered.

Notice H10 may only be activated in specific circumstances such as:

- A Mass Rogue party committing to major site instability
- A major security force going offline or any other circumstances when deemed necessary by AD.
  This will only go in effect with the authorization from both Ethics and Security Head.
  The start and end of the protocol is to be announced on the site-wide PA.

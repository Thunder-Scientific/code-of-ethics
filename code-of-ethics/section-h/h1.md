# Notice H1 | LAD Exemptions

The following is a list of exemptions agreed upon by LAD, EC, and AD. Actors are given these exemptions out of trust, to allow for more freedom while in-character and non-combative.

Violation of this trust — including, but not limited to, using these exemptions to cause major site instability, acting out of character, or acting as a combative character — will result in strikes and/or infractions. Any exempted violation of the CoE must align with how the character would act.

## Section B

- Rule B1 - Clearance Levels & Checkpoints
  - ~~Actors may enter U4 if permitted by JU. May also be classified as staff with checkpoint or U4 authorization.~~
  - Actors may roam the surface freely, provided there are no threats at the time. Actors must still close the surface gate behind themselves.
- Rule B3 - Weapon Handling
  - May use authorized weapons injure others.

## Section C

- Rule C1 - Physical and Mental Harm
  - Actors may put others in danger while in roleplay scenarios. Must not be disruptive to site stability.
- Rule C2 - Containment Zone Procedures
  - Actors may be in upper CISCZ without any present combatives
  - Actors may have non-immune characters "sneak" into lower CISCZ
- Rule C4 - Vehicle Operation Regulations
  - Actors may run people over

## Section E

- Rule E4 - Event Spectator Etiquette
  - Actors may disobey spectator etiquette. Actors can still be removed by force and must not return to the event afterward.

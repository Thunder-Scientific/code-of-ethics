# Rule A1 | On-Site Behaviour

All corporation personnel are expected to treat anyone with due respect at all times. All individuals, human, post-human, and deceased must be treated with a certain level of respect that one would expect others to owe them.

Certain behaviors are required to include the following:

- Personnel may not harass other individuals by [Roblox's Terms of Service,](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004647846-Roblox-Terms-of-Use "‌") these are defined by repeated actions that meet the listed criteria as violating Roblox's Terms of Service.
- Personnel must refrain from insulting or slandering individuals or groups by Roblox's Terms of Service
- Personnel must not discriminate against individuals based on religion, gender, race, sexual orientation, disability, national origin, or any other protected personal identifying information by Roblox terms of service.
- Personnel are expected to follow the [Roblox Community Guidelines](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/203313410-Roblox-Community-Standards "‌") and breaches of such guidelines are liable for infractions in addition to moderator actions.
- Personnel must show respect to the corpses of the deceased by not defiling or disrespecting the body of the deceased.
- Personnel should not participate in behavior that may be disruptive to other personnel's departmental duties.

Additional rules also apply to ensure the Corporation's site remains respectable, clean, and professional at all times for all individuals involved.

- Personnel should not hold long arguments or sensitive conversations in populated areas especially when it disrupts gameplay or site events.
- Personnel should avoid playing loud, irritating, or inappropriate/bypassed audio. You can play semi-loud music with the radio, but if the audio is too loud you should not play it.
- Personnel should take care to respect the personal space of others by not climbing, mounting, entering, or coming uncomfortably close to any person after being told to stop.

English is recommended as the official language for personnel on site and are liable to any difficulties of communication should any other language be used.

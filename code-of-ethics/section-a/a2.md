# Rule A2 | Banned Topics

The Corporation has a zero-tolerance policy for the distribution or discussion of media that includes, references, or otherwise mentions subjects or topics of a mature nature. These contents are not limited to:

- Any media and/or action referring to sexual content in any way.
  - This includes ramming, which will not be taken as a joke and will result in an instant infraction.
- Depictions and/or support for terrorist or extremist attacks/groups.
- Describing methods for suicide/self-harm, or motivating it seriously.
- Referring users to any harmful content/gore, or talking about the topics of such.

Engaging in any of the following activities may result in a minimum of a 1-week suspension or an exile depending on severity, and/or further action taken by the in-game moderation team.

Note: All of these regulations should be followed by the Roblox Community Guidelines.

# Rule C1 | Physical & Mental Harm

To ensure that minimal danger is posed, the following should be upheld in the interest of the health and safety of all site personnel:

- Personnel should not deliberately put other personnel in harm's way. This includes overall refusal to perform departmental duties that result in putting other personnel at risk.
- Non-combatants should avoid active conflict zones where security forces are engaging any hostile forces. Intentionally entering and loitering in an active conflict zone is strictly prohibited. If the active conflict zone is a breach and a non-combatant refuses to leave the area, security members may shoot and terminate the non-combatant.
- Personnel must show ID papers at the request of any combatant or semi-combatant given their team possesses them. Failure to abide by this request in a reasonable timeframe grants the requester permission to, if need be, down and wand the questioned personnel.
  - Combatives/Semi-combatives are to make it clear they are asking for ID papers before firing upon the suspected disguised personnel.
  - The above can be bypassed if you have reasonable suspicion that the person is a disguised personnel.
  - Personnel that cannot speak to each other as per the ROBLOX chat restrictions may wand suspected disguised personnel.
- During TSCZ Lockdowns, non-combatives are to remain outside of the TSCZ _unless_ it is confirmed to be safe enough to enter for duty purposes.
  - Additionally, non-combatives who do go into the TSCZ during a lockdown must listen to combatives when asked to leave for a valid reason.
    - Valid reason including but not limited to; danger still being present and other combatives able to do the job the non-combative is there for.

If a personnel consents to said actions, you maybe break certain sections of C4/B3 that relate to harming said individual. These sections include;

- B3, "Innocent individuals may not be injured with any weapon". You are allowed to punch/hurts others if they **consent** to it and you aren't causing a disturbance.
  - Disturbance may be defined as something such as, fighting in the inner TSCZ where combatives are attempting to do their job (doesn’t mean you can do it when TSCZ is understaffed)
  - This may only be allowed when there are minimal people around, and you are not violating things such as behavioral guidelines from your department.
- C4, "While operating any vehicle you must not deliberately crash it or recklessly operate it at speeds where it is likely to crash or intentionally run personnel over." Personnel may be harmed with a vehicle if you have their consent to do so. This also should not be done while others are around or if there is a risk of harming other individuals.

If you are using these exceptions to mess around while on a combative team during an active emergency, it will be a violation of the rule above, “Personnel should not deliberately put other personnel in harm's way. This includes overall refusal to perform departmental duties.”

Note: It is highly recommended to maintain a safe distance from any object or person that poses a threat to themselves or the people around them. Failure to adhere to this can result in serious injury or death at the fault of the individual.

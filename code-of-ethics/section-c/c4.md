# Rule C4 | Vehicle Operation Regulations

Vehicles may only be used for the transport of all personnel.

Personnel with a clearance level of 2 or higher within the department they are currently deployed may operate vehicles.

Some vehicles are reserved for their division/department or Group of Interest. A list of those exemptions will be listed down below. Any vehicle that is not listed here can be driven freely by authorized individuals.

- SD Carts and Security Jeeps may only be driven by combatant personnel
- The RECON Cart may only be driven by their respective members
- The Maxi Delivery Cart may only be driven by Maxiburger members
- The SO Jeep may only be driven by Special Operations members
- The Heavy Transport Truck and TTS Cart may only be driven by Technical Task Squad operatives
- The Bus and all of its counterparts may only be used for non-combatant events or rally events for transport.
- The ISB Jeep as well as the ISB Bus may only be spawned in for member of the Internal Security Bureau. ISB Agents may authorize others to use them.
- HWY-P Trucks can be freely driven. However, usage of their sirens whilst not being teamed HWY-P is prohibited.
- The ambulance may only be used by medical personnel.
- The RECON copter may only be used by RECON & RAH.

Operating any vehicle without the correct clearance or exception to do so will result in punishment. Combatives are permitted to bypass the above rule if they need to commandeer a vehicle for emergency reasons (crisis far away, hostiles attempting to steal car, chasing hostiles, etc.).

While operating any vehicle you must not deliberately crash it or recklessly operate it at speeds where it is likely to crash or intentionally run personnel over. You are also required to drive vehicles on roads or dedicated paths for vehicles and you may not drive them on pedestrian walkways or anywhere where a vehicle does not belong (vehicles may be driven into the TSCZ briefing rooms for event transportation purposes). Small vehicles like carts and buggies may be driven on walkways and other places as long as the driver of the vehicle is not being disruptive with the vehicle or a risk of harm to others by doing so.

Combatants may bypass vehicle driving regulations at their discretion. If a combatant is to violate the vehicle driving regulations they must have their sirens activated if the vehicle is equipped with them. If the combatant must act immediately and cannot activate their sirens they will not be punished as long as there is valid reason for it. Abuse of this will lead to punishment.

To avoid incidents an individual is not to:

- Stand on top of vehicles while they are being operated
- Block the path or lunge in the way of the vehicle while it’s being operated
- Shoot the vehicle unless a hostile has control of it or the owner is no longer in-game
  - The owner’s name of the vehicle is visible on the license plates located on the front and rear of the vehicle.
- Throw anything at the vehicle that will cause damage to it or cause it to crash

If someone is found driving a car without permission from the owner, it would be considered a Grand Theft Auto crime unless exigent circumstances exist such as vehicle recovery after being stolen by hostiles or moving it out of the way of the train system or roadway. Combatants may borrow vehicles of others without their permission if it’s needed for emergency purposes only.

## Train regulations

The train that spawns in the map may be used for transportation around the site. However, there is a small set of rules you must abide to when operating/going to be operating the train.

- U&M Conductors may drive the train freely (as long as they are following their rules)
- L2+ may drive the train within reason.
  - Whilst there is an ongoing emergency, any security personnel (Full-combatives) may operate the train if needed.
- The person who drives the train must be courteous to those who would like to use it to go places (You should know if you drive the train, people will want to use it to taxi around. Don't hog the train.)
- Whoever finds the train first may drive it unless;
  - Another person who would like to drive it is a conductor, in which you must hand it over to them
  - There is a large event happening such as a rally, patrol, etc. and they would like to use it
  - Combatives require it during an emergency on-site and must get somewhere quickly.
- You should not be using the train to get places you are not permitted.
- The train is to be driven carefully and at controllable speeds so that it does not crash.

## Gyrocopter regulations

The gyrocopter is a helicopter which can be obtained through the SD garage that may be used for flight around the site. Since flying is dangerous, there are regulations in place for flying the gyrocopter.

- The gyrocopter may be freely flown by combative personnel that hold an L3+ clearance.
  - Non-combative personnel that hold an L4+ clearance, an L4 equivalent in a **friendly** GoI or Intelligence personnel may fly the gyrocopter freely as well.
  - An exemption for this rule is made for Highway Patrol personnel who have completed their gyrocopter certification.
- You should not use the gyrocopter to get places you are not permitted.
- The gyrocopter may not be flown in high population areas. (E.g. TSCZ or CISCZ)
- The gyrocopter should be driven at a controllable speeds to limit crashing.
  - In the event that the gyrocopter crashes and the blades fall off, you are expected to discard of them within any bin as soon as you can.
  - If your team regulations allow for the rotor blade to be used for combat instead, then you are allowed to use it.
- The Research & Studies Department may freely use the gyrocopter for testing purposes.

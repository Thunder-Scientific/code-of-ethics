# Rule D3 | Solitary Confinement

Combatants may put rioting or excessively hostile TS in a cell for up to 20 minutes at a time depending on the situation. Without solitary confinement, securing the TSCZ would be extremely difficult as TS could cause problems immediately after they respawn. Due to limiting their capacity to perform actions certain rules apply:

- A “rioting” Test Subject is considered to be any TS who has received any amount of strikes.
  - Test subjects which riot during a System Riot event do not earn strikes for being terminated.
- A subject that has gained 3 strikes by violating D1 a total of 3 times (not necessarily being terminated) is subject to being put in solitary.
  - Strikes can be shown with the strike gun or combative may keep track of them on a notepad for each subject they see violating D1.
  - If you have access to the Strike Tracker, you are **strongly encouraged** **to use it** when are striking a Test Subject for violating D1. If you do not have access due to department restrictions, you should use `!notepad`
- If a Test Subject is found to be rioting a sufficient number of times, they will be put in Solitary Confinement for no less than 10 minutes and no more than 20 minutes at a time.
  - Non-Combatives may not mess with the Solitary Confinement timer at all.
- Strikes for Solitary Confinement have a 10-minute lifetime before being deemed invalid as long as the subject has not been rioting during those 10 minutes. Rioting is defined as being liable for termination as by D1.
- The combative who is to put a Test Subject into solitary is to be held responsible for releasing that same Test Subject on time. Failure to do so may result in consequences.
  - A 5-minute leeway should be given to allow the original combatant time to remove the Test Subject from Solitary.

Security Forces will normally follow the initial 3-strike rule unless otherwise stated by site protocols, which will evolve as additional time in Solitary Confinement progresses:

- There may be times that security forces may authorize placing a subject into solitary confinement for fewer instances of rioting.
- If a Test Subject continues to riot immediately after being released, they shall be returned to Solitary Confinement under a one-strike rule. “Immediately” is considered up to 10 minutes after being released from Solitary Confinement.

Contained Infected Subjects follow the same regulations as test subjects when it comes to solitary confinement.

---

**Heavy Solitary Confinement**

The Heavy Solitary Confinement Zone is a more secure location that requires 2 personnel to operate effectively and is to be used for cases when normal solitary confinement is not enough or if the subjects are particularly problematic. Guidelines for the use of Heavy Solitary are listed below:

- TS with an active KoS order has accumulated 2 strikes for SC
  - The KoS order itself does not count as a strike for this purpose
- TS has escaped from Solitary twice
  - There may be times when security forces may authorize Heavy Solitary for fewer escape attempts
- TS that has been placed into SC multiple times in the past 45 minutes
- A valid site protocol authorizes heavy solitary for fewer strikes than usual
- All standard Solitary rooms are filled

---

Note: Test Subjects may be downed to put them into Solitary Confinement as a last resort if it proves difficult to catch the subject running away or avoiding capture for Solitary Confinement.

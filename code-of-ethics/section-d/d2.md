# Rule D2 | TS Deployment

In order to deploy as Test Subject whilst being in a department/division you must complete the departments quota and complete the TS deployment requirement in the department/division that grants you your highest clearance. L4+ Personnel of a department/division who do not require permission to deploy on Test Subject team unless otherwise specified, may authorize people who are in a department/division to deploy as a Test Subject, even if they have not completed their quota.

Level 5+ personnel from that same department/division may order you to undeploy with valid reasoning unless a listed exception was met. A baseline for valid reasoning is the following:

- Deployment on Test Subject while they are on LoA in their departments
- Disciplinary action was taken upon personnel that involves TS deployment
- Department Co-Head+ from your highest department may force you off with their respective authority provided their reasoning does not contain any malicious intent

There is no restriction on how many times you may deploy in a week or for how long each time, however departments may set further restrictions on time and length.

Note: A baseline for these restrictions must be that departments/division must grant free TS deployment for at least **one day** upon completion of the departments/divisions quota.

Once ordered to undeploy with valid reasoning, there will be a period of 3 hours where personnel may not deploy on the Test Subject team. Once that cooldown has ended, they may not be ordered off the Test Subject team for the remainder of the day granted that they have met the requirements to deploy and are not on LoA or undergoing punishment that involves TS deployment.

- An exception is made for the Security Department's "One Life Rule" where personnel whose highest clearance comes from SD may not deploy on TS when the site EOP is Red+, or there is any other on-going emergency. Personnel who were on prior to the emergency may stay on TS for the remainder of their life. This rule also applies to members of the SD/U&M’s Security Engineering Team, if U&M or SD is their highest clearance department. The only exception to this rule is if the person is a High Class Test Subject/Steeleburger Guard+ or attending an official TSE event.

If authorized by your department, you may deploy at any time for any length of time. If the department your highest clearance is in allows this then L5+ personnel in other departments you are a member of may have you undeploy unless you have completed that department’s quota. This means permission from the next highest supersedes any orders to undeploy in departments granting lower clearance.

Note: If your highest clearance is granted through multiple departments at once, the departments whose rank is higher in accordance to the departments Chain of Authority takes priority. If the Chain of Authority is the same, the department whose member count is the highest restrictions apply.

High Class Test Subjects, Steeleburger Guard+, and Contained Infected Subject HRs may freely deploy on the Test Subject team without permission if they are in a department, however they may be forced off by their Department head or co-head to deploy on their department in accordance with the guidelines above.

- The exception for this is when they are hosting or co-hosting an official event for Test Subjects/Contained Infected Subjects.
- Trial High Class Test Subjects/Trialing Contained Infected Subject HRs are only exempt from deployment regulations when they are hosting or co-hosting an official event for Test Subjects.
- High Class Test Subjects/Steeleburger Guard+/Contained Infected Subjects HRs may be forced off by L5+ personnel in their highest clearance department if they have not completed their quota. It is important to note that they must be forced off personally and only used when necessary.

Note: In order to use the exception, proof must be shown that the HCTS/CIS HR in question are listed as either the host or co-host in the event announcement.

Department Co-Head+ are exempt from these regulations.

### TS Deployment Regulations for Lore Acting Department Members:

You may freely deploy on the Test Subject team, overriding TS deployment regulations from your other departments, if you are a member of the Lore Acting Department and you have a Test Subject acting character.

**Note:** You **MUST** be _in-character_ during the duration of your TS deployment.
If you're deploying on the Test Subject team _out of character_, you must follow TS deployment regulations from your highest ranking department.

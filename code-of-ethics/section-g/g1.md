# G1 | Infraction Expungement

In order for you to get your old infraction removed, you must request to see your infractions by an EC personnel by going into the Central Report Hub server and making an EC infraction expungement ticket. You must undergo a Voluntary Suspension to remove your infractions.

( [https://discord.gg/TAJgkwbCMK](https://discord.gg/TAJgkwbCMK "smartCard-inline")  )

The infraction may be removed if:

- The infraction does not have proof.
- The infraction is 1+ months old and is not unappealable.
- The infraction had no relevant proof.
- You did not receive an infraction during the past 30 days you are trying to expunge it in.

Note: B3 and appealable F2 & F1 voluntary suspensions are 48 and 168 hours respectively while all others able to expunged are 24 hours. If you have violated more than one CoE rule in one infraction, the expungement time will stack up, for example C1+A1 = 48 hour suspension.
Personnel may only expunge 1 infraction per week, and they may not have gained an infraction in the span of 1 month of the infraction that they are trying to expunge; in addition to this, you must hold at least an L1 clearance or be a LR+ within a GoI to be able to expunge an Infraction. (unless it’s an appealable GBL that you’re expunging)

---

**Regulations about Supervised Deployment:**

If you are undergoing a voluntary suspension to expunge an infraction then there is a system to allow you to still deploy on your department teams with certain restrictions. You must request to be supervised at all times while deployed by an EC member to monitor your behavior during the time of the voluntary suspension.

- EC Personnel can supervise both Combatants and Non-combatants.
- The one requesting a supervised deployment must do so ahead of time. EC personnel will meet them at either at EC spawn or their respective department spawn that the individual will deploy as.
- If the supervisor has been terminated by hostile forces or through other means of harm. The supervised personnel must head back to their spawn or EC spawn unless stated otherwise by the supervisor.
- If for any reason the supervisor has to leave, immediately swap to Facility Personnel or request for another supervisor to continue your supervised deployment. If none are available, then you must then swap to facility personnel until one is available.
- EC personnel may not supervise personnel who are on a mandatory suspension.

---

**Validity System**

This System will be used as a way to determine if the Supervised Deployment is a success or not.

- A warning is given when the personnel being supervised commits one of the acts listed in the warning section.
- If the personnel being supervised committed a CoE Violation during their supervision, the Supervisor will stop the supervision and infract the personnel. The voluntary suspension will be canceled and the timer will be reset on the ability to perform another expungement.
- Each warning must show evidence as to what caused them to receive it so that way the supervisor is accountable.
- If personnel receive more than 2 warnings or commit a CoE violation, the supervised deployment will be canceled the voluntary suspension will be canceled and the timer will be reset on the ability to perform another expungement.
- If the supervised deployment is successful and has been ongoing for a minimum of an hour, time from the initial suspension period may be removed with a ratio of 6 hours per 60 minutes supervised with a cap of 12 hours total.

---

**Warning Section**

This section lists possible actions that would count as a warning while under supervised deployment.

1. Faking a hit on someone with any melee weapon or needlessly swinging one.
2. Breaking any rule in the Code of Ethics
3. Straying away from the Supervisor without permission.
4. Unprofessional behavior or being a general annoyance to personnel on site through actions such as spamming the elevator button or throwing items at others

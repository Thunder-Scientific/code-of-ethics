# Rule B3 | Weapon Handling

Weapons ranging from light batons to heavy assault rifles are all part of the arsenal of certain divisions for various reasons. To ensure these arms are used appropriately, certain restrictions apply.

- You may not break or damage important breakable objects. (Drones, pipes and their valves etc.) without reason with any weapon. The acceptable reasons to destroy breakable objects are if hostiles must be terminated behind them, hostiles control the equipment or you are trying to gain quicker access through a route.
- Only buildings that completely prevent access to areas of the facility may be destroyed. Additionally structures that are not being actively used/serve no purpose that clutter hallways and rooms may be destroyed. (_Refer to D1 for specifications about TS builds in TSCZ_).
- Innocent individuals may not be injured with any weapon.
  - The exception(s) to this is if you are in the kill zone of Internal Security Bureau members or Juggernaut Unit Operatives, or if you are a Security Department member following the personal space clause found within their handbook. A baseline of these regulations follow a 5-stud stun (SD) or kill zone (ISB, JU) if they violate the personal space of the individual. There may be instances where they can terminate you for intervening with their duties after receiving a warning from them.
  - Personnel who carry it may use the blood transfusion gun to take blood from subjects inside of solitary confinement.
  - Combat Medics may use stims on personnel with their own internal regulations of consent or free-use
  - Special Operations Operatives may terminate individuals for violating their personal space following A1.
    - This being about 3 studs or on their characters head, hugging them, etc.

---

Non-combatant personnel follow additional regulations to these rules:

- Non-combatant personnel may not carry large or automatic weaponry unless given special authorization from the Administrative Department (Administrative Intern+ or through an executive order such as H10 when active.)
- Non-combatants may not harm hostiles or use weapons unless they are doing so in self-defense or are protecting other defenseless personnel (Downed combatants, unarmed non-combatant personnel…) from being attacked or threatened with harm.
  - Note that your priority should be escaping the threat if there is a safe way to do so. You may not constantly defend others or run after someone to finish them off since your duty is to escape the danger.
  - If you have downed a hostile in self-defense in an appropriate way, you are permitted to finish them off.
- Non-combatants may fire upon hostiles that have taken control over a vehicle that belongs to them and chase the hostile in order to retrieve it. If you have downed the hostile in the process of it, you are allowed to finish them off.
- Non-combatants may not take any weaponry that is unavailable for them to use in their firearm permit. They are only allowed to use weapons available to them when they spawn or in their department headquarters unless stated otherwise by their departmental command (L5+).
- Additionally, when on the Facility Personnel team you may only use weapons if you have an issued firearm permit.
- In the case that you are a Contained Infected Personnel (CIP) with over 200  Health, you may not wield any firearms exept for the TASER. Note that you must have a Type-A Firearm permit to use the TASER.

Personnel who receive / purchase unauthorized weaponry. Any black market item, items from John Recycle, or weaponry from hostile forces that is not normally available to you in your standard loadout, may not use it and should surrender it to a combatant or administrator, or they should dispose of it as soon as possible.

Combatants cannot use weapons surrendered to them or weapons they have picked up unless they have access to them normally or are authorized to use them.

### Manual turret

The manual turret can be purchased from the SDHQ garage, it is only to be used by full combatives, additionally these rules must be followed:

- No more than 2 turret mays be welded to any 1 vehicle.
- Turrets may not be taken into spawn areas (TSCZ, CISCZ, SDHQ, etc.)
  - The exception to this is in TSCZ, where up to 2 turrets may be placed in inner TSCZ behind the red line.
- Turrets are not to be left lying around unattended as hostiles can take them.
- Semi combatives may use the turrets on Red+.

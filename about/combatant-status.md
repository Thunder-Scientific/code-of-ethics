# Combatant Status

The purpose of this section is to serve as information regarding the combatant status of TSC personnel when they are deployed on a certain department, as well as the combatant status of GoI members when they are operating on-site. This is a general basis which departments go off from, they can stray away from it but must stay reasonable for their changes.

The following sections will explain all positions which personnel are assigned when deployed on a specific team or department. Any exceptions for subdivisions (like the Technical Task Squad) will be listed and explained in detail in this section as well.

**Combatant**

All personnel which possess the status of being a combatant are site security personnel and are expected to respond to any and all threats on-site to protect civil personnel. They may engage hostiles freely as long as the criteria is met to do so.

The following list of departments, Groups of Interest are deemed Combatant:

- Security Department
- Combat Medics
- Technical Task Squad are considered full combatants but may only act as such in S1 and CTZ. Additionally, TTS may act as full combatants in S2 under the conditions listed within their handbook: [https://trello.com/c/nmYUka9k/65-1-4-sector-permissions](https://trello.com/c/nmYUka9k/65-1-4-sector-permissions "smartCard-inline")
- Security Engineering Team (SET)
- Recontainment Unit (Asset Handlers)
- RAH (Assets)
- Special Operations
- Juggernaut Unit

**Semi-Combatant**

All personnel which possess the status of being a Semi-Combative are defensive personnel which only engage hostiles in protection of others and themselves. This status is mostly given to intelligence personnel.

While Semi-Combatants are to stay defensive, if a situation gets out of hand and the site is deemed in high danger (Code RED+ and specified scenarios in the respective entity with EC approval) they may elevate to combatant status to assist combatants.

Semi-Combatants follow the same regulations in engagement with hostiles outside of their combatant status, however they may chase down any hostile which poses a threat and/or engaged with them to terminate them.

Semi-Combatants may freely terminate disguised personnel for the duty of internal security on-site.

The following list of departments, Groups of Interest are deemed Semi-Combatants:

- Internal Security Bureau
  - ISB have their own written regulations regarding their combatant status. This can be found on their Master Guidelines.
- Ethics Committee Rogue Response
- Highway Patrol

Semi-combatants may not participate in hostile raids. (Unless the department/division has gotten permission from the hostile group to engage in raids.)

**Non-Combatant**

All personnel which possess the status of being a non-combatant are civil personnel which may only engage hostiles for self-defense of themselves or other non-combatant personnel as stated in section B3 of the Code of Ethics.

The following list of departments, Groups of Interest are deemed non-combatant:

- Research & Studies
- Medical Department
- Utility & Maintenance
- RAH Assets are an extension of RECON and work together with assigned handlers to deal with breaches and protection of CTZ. The default state of assets is to be non-combative in CTZ and only attack hostiles if they enter specimen chambers or are engaged by hostiles. If an Asset is paired with a handler, they gain full combative status.
- Utility & Maintenance Specialized Branch
- External Relations
- Ethics Committee
- Facility Personnel
- Administrative Department
- Manufacturing Department teams (Site Engineer, Quality Assurance, etc.)

Hostile Groups of Interest may go on peacekeeping to become neutral with TSC for a certain amount of time. Whenever peacekeeping is in effect, their units are to follow the Code of Ethics and refer to this page for information regarding their on-site combatant status. Hostile GoI units which can go on peacekeeping are marked with a * at the end of the unit's name. Hostile GoI members who are deployed to bodyguard non-combatant members of their own GoI are assigned semi-combatant status. For the time they are deployed on-site, they are to follow the Code of Ethics and to be considered non-hostile.

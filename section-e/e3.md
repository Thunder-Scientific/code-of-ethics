# Rule E3 | Misusing Scientific Equipment

Scientific equipment is the equipment found around the facility (such as the centrifuge, instance 294 (coffee machine), the acid pit, radiation chamber, static chamber, hadron collider, vehicle testing chamber, incinerator, and Unit 1 Labs.) and is meant for scientific testing and scientific testing alone except instance 294.

Misuse of scientific equipment would be:

- Using the equipment without the supervision of a scientist.
- Testing on the equipment without being a scientist.
- Using the equipment as a scientist during an unethical test.
- Abusing the equipment as a scientist in hopes of breaking it during a test.
- Abusing equipment as a non-scientist in hopes of sabotaging a test.
- Using the TSCZ testing queue buzzer outside of testing conditions.

These rules only apply to staff members and external combatants. (Security forces are counted as staff members.)

U&M may utilize the incinerator to dispose of trash bags.

Specialists may use testing equipment outside of testing permeters.

- Specialist Instructors+ are permitted to allow CIS Analysts to use testing equipment within a specialist event.

**There are more regulations regarding instance 294 in rule C1.**

_Until the Medical Department gets its equipment, they will be counted as scientists when doing medical tests._
_Veterinarian Program personnel may freely use vials for rehabilitation purposes._

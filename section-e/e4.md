# Rule E4 | Event Spectator Etiquette

Due to the nature of site events and potential dangers encountered by testing, certain regulations are in place to assist the essential employees of the corporation in performing their events properly.

To help the process go smoothly and reduce the overall danger, a list of restricted actions for personnel accompanying the site event would be as follows:

- Personnel should stand at least 4 studs away from the windows in an observation room during an active test event.
- Personnel must refrain from opening or closing doors that have not been specifically instructed/allowed to open or close during an active test event unless it's required to participate in spectating the test. Opening the containment doors without special instruction during a test event, or afterward with present specimen or subjects inside, is strictly prohibited.
- Accompanying Personnel should not stand in between the subjects and the event host to avoid confusion in the subjects and so the event may be communicated clearly. Security personnel may freely do so for briefing and searching subjects. Security may also do so if the event host is being threatened by the subjects or if directed by the event host.
  - Spectators must not stand between the subjects and either the event host or security escort at any time unless it's required.
  - Spectators must not interrupt the event by attempting to speak to the subjects or being in the way of the event host while they are trying to communicate.
    - Being a general annoyance to the event host may result in your dismissal from spectating the event.

---

Personnel spectating any site event must ask for permission to do so from the event host unless the host states that anyone may freely spectate. The event host may also at any time dismiss you from spectating the event depending on departmental regulations that limit them or additional concerns from the host such as event interruption.

The following individuals may spectate freely unless found to violate any of the above-restricted actions or they are restricted by the security clearance required in special cases for Unit-4 testing:

- Administrative Staff
- ISB
- EC Board Agent+ (EC Board Member+ for Unit-4 tests)
- Supervisors to the event host

Failure to follow all instructions from the security escort or perform any of the listed restricted actions may result in your removal from the event.

# Rule D4 | Contained Infected Subject Procedures

### Contained Infected Subject and Infected Subject Information

Contained Infected Subjects are people hired to act as infected on-site. These users are the ones who are seen as infected’s in the Contained Infected Subject Containment Zone, and have been trained to act out being a character of their choosing. They can either be OOC (Out of Character) or IC (In-character) both of which have different regulations. OOC CIS would have their username showing as well as their CIS rank in their rank-tag. IC CIS will have “CIS-XXXX” and not have a rank-tag visible.
The other type of users you may see are people with the tags that only have “Infected” in them. These are Test Subjects that have not been hired to act, and are from tests that either an RSD or MD hosted; alongside this VP may take CIS to various locations within the site if escort ratios are followed. If within the CISCZ, escort rules can be relaxed, given a free-roam is authorized. VP may not take CIS to Sector 2 given alarm triggers and regulation contradictions. Rehabilitation events are ethically valid as long as a VP follows the requests of a CIS given they do not harm anyone. This is stated here to not cause confusion.

Summary:
IC = In-character (Acting as a character)
OOC = Out-of-Character (Not acting as any character)
IS = Infected Subject (an infected TS, not an actor)

While the rules below should be followed by all combatives, when dealing with infected individuals, you should attempt to find an immune combative to handle the situation if you are not immune.

---

### General Policy

When confronted with a CIS, you should not immediately start firing unless they are actively showing hostility towards you. (Shooting first, running at you with fists, etc.)

#### Encountering an IC CIS

IC CIS will normally be passive when encountered all have different reactions to personnel due to them being in-character. With them, you should also not fire immediately similar to the OOC encounter guidelines. However, instead of going straight to leading them back, you are allowed to make conversation with the subjects. Perhaps to find out why they may have escaped, if they would like to go back, stuff that will make the interaction more intriguing for both parties.
If you are not one for acting or interacting with the CIS, you may ask for an immune or another combative to handle the situation.
The CIS is to be brought back to CISCZ but more preferably with an immune/QIE (Qualified Infected Escort). If none are available at the time, you may cautiously bring the CIS back to containment.
When brought back to CISCZ, you may ask them to hand over any contraband they may have or check them with the wand before they are let back in.

- Items that are given through the “faction” system are not to be considered contraband if the CIS is wearing the appropriate uniform.
- Guns, such as the “Model-870” are still to be considered contraband.

In summary:

- Assess whether or not CIS is hostile
  - Hostile; attempt to have them stand down before firing upon them.
    - Hostility can be shown as harming you or others and running at you with any weapon (including fists).
    - Singular acts of hostility do not count as “hostile”.  Continuous acts do.
  - Passive; take time to interact with the subject. Ask for them to drop any contraband they may have.
    - If non-compliant, you are **NOT** to shoot at the CIS. Try to speak to them, or allow another combative to handle the situation.
- Call for an immune
  - No immunes; escort the CIS back to CISCZ without cuffs.
  - Immune response; have them take the CIS back and continue normal duties.

If the CIS you encounter has special abilities that would make recontaining more difficult, (Tiger shark speed, hypnosis, Squog endurance, etc.) you are able to take less precautions when handling the situation.
This means, you are able to be less lenient when assessing for hostility or non-compliance.

#### Encountering an OOC CIS / Chamber Infected

- OOC CIS follow all termination guidelines inside of D1 essentially making them TS, they will be KOS outside of CISCZ
- Chamber Infected will also follow all termination guidelines inside of D1, but will only be KOS outside of their respective chambers.

### **Infected Subject Termination Policies**

These subjects are not to be treated as lightly as actors. They are considered TS still and have termination policies similar to the ones stated in D1. These are the termination guidelines for Infected subjects.

- Showing hostility (harming any other person or subject, infecting personnel)
- Inside of the any ventilation, including BM
- Wielding any contraband
- Outside of inner CISCZ (On the road is considered OUTER CISCZ and may be terminated there)
- Using any vehicle
- Stacking more then 3 players (Punching bag counts as 1 player)
- Found in any area they are not authorized to be
  - Upper CISCZ or onto the ramp. They may be there if they have specific permission
- Caught breaking the cover in the ball pit
- Caught attempting to open BM vent
- Going onto rafters or any high places (following climbing rules)

Infected subjects that are within the CISCZ without the proper Immune ratios being met are permitted to be terminated; as they create a huge risk factor.

---

#### Climbing Rules

- Any CIS may use the vent into the spider wolf den, which is behind a bookcase in inner CISCZ. They are allowed to stay in the room it leads to, but cannot go into rafters itself.
- Any CIS may climb onto the rafters located in the pool section.

### Notes

CIS (both **OOC and IC**) that are currently under any long-lasting visual or statistic-altering effects (from any testing equipment such as the vending machine, static chamber, etc.) **outside of testing** are allowed to be terminated. (Examples of such effects being: extra health, height changes, etc.)
Before termination, you **must** have a reasonable suspicion that they are under the effects of the vending machine contents if you are not able to properly evaluate their stats. (Similar to the D1 regulation regarding TS and 294)
Ways to check include; medical personnel’s “Health Analyzer”, observing any speed increase/decrease, or observing whether they have grown/shrunk in height.

These are base guidelines for combatives who encounter any kind of IS/CIS. The immune combatives, (RECON) are to follow their guidelines that are stated in their handbook.
